Motion Designer & Code Generator

Load your CAD parts, place the motors, pose and name movements, then generate code for your board.

1 · CAD parts

A browser can't open SolidWorks files (.sldprt/.sldasm). In SolidWorks use File → Save As → STL (binary). For an assembly, untick “Save all components of an assembly in a single file” so each part saves as its own STL and keeps its position. Then load one STL per moving part. If the export loses the assembly positions or mates, rebuild them in step 3.

2 · Model

Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Orange dots are motor pivots (the selected one is red).

3 · Assembly mates

Only needed if your STL export lost the assembly positions or mates. Choose a mate type, press Pick, then click a face on the model. Flat and cylindrical faces are detected automatically. Mate each new part to one that is already placed, starting from the base, because mates are applied in order.

A hinge mate defines a rotation axis and pivot. Press “Make motor” on it to create a motor that rotates the moving part about that axis (it follows the hinge if you edit the mates).

4 · Motors

Pose slider angles are relative to the home pose (0°). Servos treat 0° as their centre.
