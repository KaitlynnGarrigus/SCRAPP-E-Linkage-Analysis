Press Listen and just talk, or type a sentence.

Listen keeps the mic open and the face reacts as you speak. With Tired mode on, SCRAPP-E falls asleep (showing your reaction named “Tired”) after 1 minute without speech; say “SCRAPP-E” (like “Scrappy”) to wake it. Speech recognition runs in your browser (Chrome/Edge) and may send audio to a cloud service; tone is a rough pitch/loudness check.

Test on a GME12864-13 OLED

The module is a 128×64 monochrome OLED. Your 24×12 eye grid is drawn at 5×5 pixels per cell (120×60), centred, so what you draw is what shows on the screen. Pick your board and generate a ready-to-upload test program with every saved reaction in it.

Live preview of the OLED (current face)

I couldn't find a datasheet for the exact -13 variant. Most 0.96″ four-pin GME12864 boards are SSD1306 over I2C at 0x3C, so that is the default. Upload the I2C scanner first to confirm the address and wiring. If the picture is shifted by 2 pixels, switch the controller to SH1106. If your module has 7 pins it is an SPI version, which this tool doesn't generate yet.
