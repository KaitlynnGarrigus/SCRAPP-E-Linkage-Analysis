Press Listen and just talk, or type a sentence.

Listen keeps the mic open and the face reacts as you speak. With Tired mode on, SCRAPP-E falls asleep (showing your reaction named “Tired”) after 1 minute without speech; say “SCRAPP-E” (like “Scrappy”) to wake it. Speech recognition runs in your browser (Chrome/Edge) and may send audio to a cloud service; tone is a rough pitch/loudness check.
