Joints & gearboxes

Motor torque is at the motor shaft. Joint mass is the actuator mass sitting at that joint. Gearbox efficiencies and ratio ranges are typical values — replace with your datasheet numbers.

2D model

 Each joint sweeps min↔max at its gearbox output speed × animation speed. Dots show the reachable workspace. When paused, drag any joint or the tip to pose the arm (limits respected).

Graphs

Stress is bending only, σ = M·c/I, at the root of each link using the joint torque as the moment; strain = σ/E. Time graphs record while the animation plays.

Results

Torques are static (gravity only) — no inertia, friction or dynamic loads. Peak is the max over the joint limits (sampled). Use the safety factor to cover the rest.
